# Simple Punjabi — Support

Simple Punjabi is an offline app for learning spoken Punjabi with useful everyday sentences, written in English letters.

## Common questions

**Does the app need the internet?**  
No. It works fully offline after installation.

**Is this Gurmukhi script?**  
No. All prompts and answers use English letters (roman script). The app focuses on speaking and understanding Punjabi.

**Where is my progress saved?**  
On your device only. Deleting the app clears your progress.

**Which dialect of Punjabi?**  
The course is closest to Malwai Punjabi from Eastern Punjab.
