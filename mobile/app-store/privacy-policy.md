# Simple Punjabi — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: August 2026*

Sachin Chopra ("I") built Simple Punjabi as a free app. This page explains how the app handles information.

## Summary

Simple Punjabi does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. Everything stays on your device.

## Information we do not collect

Simple Punjabi does not require an account. The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location. It does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves your learning progress and settings locally on your iPhone using on-device storage, including:

- Level medals and scores
- Mute preference
- Question direction (English → Punjabi or Punjabi → English)

This data never leaves your device and is not backed up to any server operated for this app. Deleting the app removes this data from your device.

## Internet access

Simple Punjabi works fully offline. The app does not need an internet connection to function. The in-app About panel may include a link to external websites (for example, Wikipedia). Opening those links is optional and governed by those sites' policies.

## Text-to-speech

Optional audio uses Apple's built-in text-to-speech on your device. The app does not record or transmit your voice. The app does not use the microphone.

## Children

Simple Punjabi is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
